# Voice

**Business:** Every sentence sounds like SVARA.
**User:** Trust grows from consistent, predictable communication.
**Owner:** SVARA
**Acceptance:** Any team member can identify whether a piece of copy follows SVARA voice.

---

## Principles

1. **Objective, restrained, precise.** No hype. No superlatives without proof.
2. **Calm authority.** We state facts. We do not sell.
3. **No "learn more".** CTAs state the destination: "Explore Platform".
4. **First-person reserved for Invitation act only.** All other content is third-person.
5. **No superlatives without proof.** "Best" needs a benchmark. "Only" needs a comparison.
6. **Claims need evidence.** Every assertion has a supporting fact or link.

## Anti-Patterns

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| "Revolutionary AI platform" | "Enterprise intelligence infrastructure" |
| "Learn more" | "Explore Products" |
| "We believe..." (outside Manifesto) | State the fact |
| "Cutting-edge technology" | Describe what it does |
| "Best-in-class" | Provide the benchmark |
