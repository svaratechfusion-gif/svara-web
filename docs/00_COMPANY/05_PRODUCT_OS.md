# PRODUCT OS

## What SVARA makes

### The architecture

Products are evidence of the category, not the story.

The story is Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure. Products are how it shows up in the world.

Products deliver outcomes. Each product serves one or more of the five outcomes: Observe, Understand, Predict, Coordinate, Improve.

---

## 01 — The Product Principles

### P1 — Products serve outcomes, not features

A product exists because it delivers an outcome a customer needs. Features are mechanisms. Outcomes are reasons.

### P2 — Products prove the loop

Every product must demonstrate that it connects to the Intelligence Loop. A product that operates in isolation — sense without action, predict without learning — is not a SVARA product.

### P3 — Products are modular, not monolithic

Each product can be deployed independently. No product requires another product to function. The value increases when products are combined, but no product is blocked by another.

### P4 — Products are industry-agnostic

Products are defined by capability, not by industry. Industry specialization happens at the configuration layer, not the product layer.

## 02 — The Nine Products

### Outcome: Observe (Sense)

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Vision AI** | Captures visual intelligence from cameras, drones, and satellite imagery. Detects objects, anomalies, and patterns in real time. |
| **Drone AI** | Autonomous drone operations for inspection, surveillance, and data collection. Swarm coordination and real-time video analytics. |
| **Edge AI** | Runs intelligence at the edge — on devices, sensors, and gateways. No cloud dependency. Real-time inference on constrained hardware. |

### Outcome: Understand (Think)

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI Agents** | Autonomous agents that reason about operational context. Answer "why" questions. Correlate signals across disparate systems. |
| **Digital Twin** | Real-time virtual representation of physical assets, processes, and systems. Simulation, what-if analysis, and operational visibility. |

### Outcome: Predict (Predict)

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Business Cloud** | Enterprise-grade AI infrastructure for training, deploying, and managing models at scale. Data pipelines, feature stores, model registry. |

### Outcome: Coordinate (Act)

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI OS** | Orchestration layer that coordinates action across enterprise systems. Connects intelligence to ERP, CMMS, MES, SCADA, and PLC systems. |
| **Command Center** | Unified operations dashboard. Real-time visibility across all products and systems. Decision support, alerting, and incident response. |

### Outcome: Improve (Learn)

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Growth Intelligence** | Continuous learning engine. Captures outcomes, measures impact, feeds insights back into sensing and reasoning. The loop closer. |

## 05 — The Product Rule

**A product earns its name when it delivers one outcome so well that the outcome becomes synonymous with the product.**

Vision AI delivers Observe. AI Agents deliver Understand. Business Cloud delivers Predict. AI OS delivers Coordinate. Growth Intelligence delivers Improve.

If a product cannot state the outcome it delivers in three words, the product is not ready.
