# Manifesto

**Business:** State SVARA's beliefs.
**User:** Decide if they share our worldview.
**Owner:** SVARA
**Acceptance:** A reader can decide whether SVARA's philosophy aligns with their own.

---

We believe enterprise intelligence should be infrastructure, not an add-on.

Every system should learn. Every decision should be informed. Every operation should be continuously optimized.

The enterprise of tomorrow runs on one intelligence layer — not fifty disconnected tools.

We are building that layer.
