# Milestones

Each milestone is defined by what the visitor understands — not by what ships.

---

## Milestone 1 — Homepage Experience

**Outcome:** A first-time visitor should understand, within 60 seconds, that:

1. Enterprises generate enormous amounts of disconnected operational intelligence.
2. Fragmentation — not data scarcity — is the real problem.
3. SVARA is an Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure, not another SaaS tool.
4. SVARA's products work together as one coordinated ecosystem.
5. The company is technically credible and enterprise-focused.

**Success:** The visitor can state all five outcomes after the first scroll-through.
