## Rule

Every asset, every page, every feature traces through this tree.

A product page sits under its capability, under the platform, under the category. An industry page shows which capability applies. The homepage names the category, teaches the framework, shows the platform, demonstrates capabilities, and proves with industries.

If it doesn't fit in this tree, SVARA doesn't do it.
