## Investment Thesis

Enterprise operational data is growing faster than human capacity to interpret it. SVARA replaces disconnected point tools with one intelligence infrastructure that sees, reasons, predicts, and automates across every system.

## Market Opportunity

The global enterprise AI infrastructure market. Organizations running legacy ERP, CRM, IoT, and operational systems that were never designed to share data.

## Business Model

Enterprise SaaS. Platform + product subscriptions. Deployment options: cloud, on-premises, hybrid.
