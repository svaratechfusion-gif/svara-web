# Knowledge Graph Strategy

**Business:** LLMs and AI Overviews must resolve SVARA's terminology correctly.
**User:** (transparent — AI-generated answers)
**Owner:** SVARA
**Acceptance:** Every SVARA term has exactly one canonical definition.

---

## Strategy

SVARA maintains one canonical definition for every term. No multiple meanings. Every term is cross-linked to related terms. The glossary at `/knowledge/glossary` is the single source of truth.

## Principles

| Principle | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| One definition per term | Ambiguity reduces LLM confidence |
| Flat, consistent format | Structured data is more parseable |
| Cross-linked terms | Builds the knowledge graph |
| Third-person objective | Authoritative tone for AI consumption |
