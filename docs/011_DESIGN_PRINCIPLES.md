# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Owner:** Product Team | **Review Date:** 2026-10-08
**Dependencies:** DOCUMENT 001 | **Related:** DOCUMENT 005, DOCUMENT 010

## Laws, not guidelines. Every decision traces to a principle.

---

## Principle 001 — Teach before selling

The visitor must understand the category before they understand SVARA. Every scene, page, and interaction teaches first. Selling is the result of teaching, not the goal of it.

## Principle 002 — Motion must communicate

Every animation explains a concept. If removing an animation does not reduce understanding, the animation is decoration. Decoration does not ship.

## Principle 003 — Every scroll changes understanding

Scrolling is not navigation. It is revelation. Each scroll position reveals something the visitor did not know before. If scroll does not change understanding, the scene is not ready.

## Principle 004 — One viewport. One message.

A viewport contains exactly one message. If a viewport contains two messages, split them. If a viewport contains zero messages, delete it.

## Principle 005 — Remove anything that doesn't build trust

Enterprise buyers trust substance. Animations, gradients, particles, transitions — if they do not build trust, they are noise. Noise is removed.

## Principle 006 — Complex technology. Simple explanation.

The technology is sophisticated. The explanation is clear. Every visitor should understand what SVARA does within one scroll-through. If they cannot, the explanation failed, not the technology.

## Principle 007 — Animation is evidence. Not decoration.

An animation proves a concept. Particles prove data flows. Camera movement proves scale. Lighting proves transformation. If the animation does not prove something, it does not exist.

## Principle 008 — Enterprise buyers reward clarity. Not spectacle.

Clarity is the premium experience. Spectacle is a distraction. Enterprise buyers have seen every animation technique. What they have not seen is a clear explanation of a complex category. Clarity wins.

## Principle 009 — Every page answers one question.

A page that answers two questions confuses. A page that answers zero questions wastes time. Every page has exactly one primary question, and every element on that page serves that question.

## Principle 010 — Everything supports the category.

SVARA is not building a company. SVARA is building a category. Every scene, page, animation, and interaction exists to establish Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure as the most important category in enterprise technology. If it does not support the category, it does not belong.
